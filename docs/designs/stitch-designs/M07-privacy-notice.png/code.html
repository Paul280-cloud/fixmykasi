Privacy Notice

Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) Notice · Effective 2026
Core Privacy Principle

"We collect your name, email and, if you choose, your phone number, so you can log in and report faults."

What we collect

Under POPIA, we collect only the minimum personal information required to deliver municipal fault reporting and dispatch:

Data item Required? Purpose
Full Name Yes Account identity
Email address Yes Login & alerts
Phone number Optional SMS updates
Ward selection Yes Ward queue routing
Fault photos & GPS When reporting Crew dispatch

Why we need it

Your details are used strictly to verify resident identity, prevent duplicate fault spam in your ward, let you track repair milestones from dispatch to confirmation, and notify you when your community issue is resolved.

Who can see it

Public view: Fault descriptions, photos, and street addresses are public so neighbours can upvote and avoid duplicate reports. Your name and contact details are NEVER public.
Municipal staff: Ward admins and assigned field teams (contractors or municipal crews) see your name and contact details solely to clarify fault location or access.

Where it is stored

Cross-Border Hosting Notice (PRD 12.4 & POPIA Section 72)

Our cloud database and servers are securely hosted outside South Africa in enterprise European/US data centres that comply with international data security standards (ISO 27001, SOC 2) and offer protections equivalent to POPIA. All personal data in transit and at rest is encrypted.

How long we keep it

We retain your account profile while your account is active. Resolved fault records and community upvote counts remain stored in the public ward history for civic audit records, but your personal identifiers are detached upon account deletion.

How to delete your account

You have full control over your data. You can delete your account at any time:

S11 Profile & Settings Delete account

Deleting your account immediately purges your credentials, phone number, and upvote history. Faults you reported stay public without your name because neighbours rely on repair tracking (M5).
