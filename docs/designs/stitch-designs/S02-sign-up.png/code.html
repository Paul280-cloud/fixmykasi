S2 Sign Up Implementation & Specification Compliance Notes

Form Fields & Validation Rules (PRD 8.2)

  • Full name: Required, 2 to 60 characters. Verbatim error: "Enter your full name."
  • Email: Required, lowercase format. Verbatim errors: "Enter a valid email address." and "This email already has an account. Log in instead."
  • Phone number: Optional, +27 format. Verbatim error: "Enter a South African number, like +27 82 123 4567."
  • Home ward: Required dropdown. Verbatim error: "Choose your ward." Filled with Joburg townships (e.g. Ward A: Orlando West).
  • Password: Required, 8+ chars with letter & number + eye toggle. Verbatim error: "Use at least 8 characters with a letter and a number."
  • Age Checkbox (18+): Verbatim error: "You must be 18 or older to use FixMyKasi."
  • Privacy Notice: Links to M7. Verbatim error: "Please agree to the Privacy Notice to continue."

Layout & State Compliance (PRD 8.2 & 9.1)

  • Header (8.3): Heritage Brown (#531A17) with white back arrow (returns to S1) and 3px Gold (#D8A851) underline.
  • Visitor Tab Bar: Exactly 2 tabs: Faults and Log in (active). Preserved across all frames.
  • Empty State: Omitted as specified in PRD Section 8.2 ("Empty: not needed, because the form is the content").
  • Loading State: "Create account" button shows animated spinner and becomes disabled.
  • Error State: Top general error banner verbatim: "Something went wrong. Try again." placed above the button. Preserves all user input values.
  • Offline State: Top grey offline banner ("You're offline. Changes will not be saved.") with "Create account" disabled (#D0D5DD / #667085).
  • Success Redirect: Routes to S3 with the verbatim banner: "Check your email to confirm your account."

Missing Items & TODOs:

All required elements, fields, labels, placeholders, errors, and interaction flows were fully and unambiguously specified in PRD Sections 8.2, 8.3, and 9.1. No additional fields were invented; no grey placeholder TODO boxes required.
