S9 Ward Dashboard Specification & Compliance Notes

Ordered Layout & Chrome Enforcement (PRD 8.2, 8.3 & BR15)

  • Header: Heritage Brown (#531A17) with 3px solid Gold line (#D8A851). Subtitle badge indicates designated ward (Ward A: Orlando West).
  • Core Stat Cards (PRD 8.3): Open faults (value 4), Overdue faults (value 1 in #B42318 with animated pulse clock), and Average time to fix (5.0 days) calculated over the last 30 days as prescribed by BR15.
  • Category Breakdown: Visual progress bars for all 6 standard municipal categories (Electricity at 75%, Clean water at 25%, Roads at 0%, Sewer at 0%, Waste at 0%, Public spaces at 0%).
  • Secondary Action: 48px touch target button "View overdue faults (1)" that navigates to S8 Ward Queue with the "Overdue only" filter chip active.
  • Persistent Navigation: Tab bar with Dashboard highlighted in South African Coat of Arms Green (#005D28) and Ward Admin ZK avatar.

State Coverage & Business Rules Verification

  • State 1 (Normal): Shows active telemetry with 4 open faults, 1 overdue fault, and category proportional fills.
  • State 2 (Empty): Displays celebratory green banner, 0 open faults, 0 overdue, and verbatim copy: "No faults fixed in the last 30 days." for average fix time.
  • State 3 (Loading): 3 skeleton cards, 6 skeleton category bar rows, and 1 skeleton action button in smooth CSS pulse animation.
  • State 4 (Error): Centered warning icon, verbatim message "Something went wrong. Try again.", and 48px primary "Try again" button in brand green.
  • State 5 (Offline): Top charcoal grey banner "You're offline. Changes will not be saved.", offline status bar badge, and cached timestamp "Last updated 12 Nov 2026, 10:15".

MISSING ITEMS & TODOS: All required elements, metrics, categories, states, and seed data were fully provided in PRD Section 8.2, 8.3, 8.4, and BR15. No TODO placeholder boxes were required.
