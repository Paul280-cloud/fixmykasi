S6 Report a Fault Implementation & Specification Compliance Notes

Layout, Ordered Components & Business Rules

  • Header: Heritage Brown (#531A17) bar with white back arrow (≥44px touch target) and 3 px Gold (#D8A851) underline.
  • Emergency Notice (Yellow Box): Verbatim text "If someone's life is in danger, call 10177 or 112 from a cellphone." with warning triangle icon.
  • Photo Tiles (BR2): 3 photo slots with "Add photo" trigger (camera/gallery support) and verbatim checkbox "It is not safe to take a photo".
  • AI Suggestion Chip (BR20 / F7): Appears directly beneath photos when an image is selected, suggesting detected category/fault (e.g. "Burst clean water pipe") with one-tap apply button.
  • Form Fields & Validation:
    • Category & Fault Type: 6 categories (Clean water, Sewer, Roads, Electricity, Waste, Parks) from PRD 10.4.
    • "This is dangerous" (BR19): Checkbox that auto-ticks and locks for auto-urgent faults (like high pressure burst clean water pipes).
    • Ward: Defaults automatically to resident's home ward (Ward A: Orlando West).
    • Use my location (BR3): GPS toggle defaulting to On with Street address and optional Landmark fields.
    • Description (BR2, BR17): Character counter 0 / 500. Enforces 10 character minimum (30 chars when no photo is attached).
  • Similar Reports Workflow (BR4 / M6): Primary button starts as "Next" to check for duplicate reports nearby, transitioning to "Submit report" once cleared.

State Coverage & Role Enforcement (PRD 8.2 & 7.2)

  • Normal State: Initial clean visit for verified Resident; home ward defaulted to Ward A, location toggled on.
  • State 1B (Sample Content): Populated with verbatim seed data: Photo of water flowing across the road, Clean water → Burst clean water pipe, dangerous locked, Ward A, 23 Vilakazi Street, landmark Near the Mandela House, isiZulu description "Amanzi aphuma epayipini kusukela izolo ekuseni".
  • Empty State: Marked as not needed per PRD Section 8.2 ("because the form is the content").
  • Loading State: Submit button disabled displaying animated spinner with "Uploading photos…" and real-time percentage progress bar.
  • Error State Variants:
    • Verbatim banner: "Upload failed. Check your connection." with actionable "Try again" button.
    • Verbatim daily limit: "You can report up to 5 faults a day. Please try again tomorrow."
    • Preserves all resident inputs across errors.
  • Offline State: Top shared grey offline banner ("You're offline. Your report will be saved when you're back online.") and disabled submit button (#D0D5DD / #667085).
  • Success State: Redirects to S5 Fault Details showing verbatim toast: "Fault reported. Your reference number is FMK-2026-00125."
  • Role-based Tab Bar: Persistent Resident tab bar (Faults, Report [Active], My reports, Profile with initials badge TM for Thandeka Mokoena).

Missing Items & TODOs:

All required elements, fields, labels, placeholders, errors, and seed data states were fully and unambiguously specified in PRD Section 8.2, 8.3, 8.4, and 9.1. No missing elements or placeholder TODO boxes were required.
