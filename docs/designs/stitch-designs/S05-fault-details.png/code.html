S5 Fault Details Implementation & Specification Compliance Notes

Component & Layout Compliance (PRD 8.2 & 8.3)

  • Header: Heritage Brown (#531A17) with white back arrow (≥44px touch target), white title with verbatim reference number (FMK-2026-00123), and persistent 3 px gold (#D8A851) underline.
  • Photo Carousel: Top media container showing resident photos with counter pill (1 / 2). Supports F6 "Before" and "After" labelled groups for Fixed/Closed states.
  • Fault Type & Status Chip: Bold title, status chip dot with written name (Scheduled: #87451A), and verbatim Urgent label (#EF4718 background with dark #1C1C1C text & warning icon).
  • Location: Street address, landmark, and ward indicator.
  • Description (F7): Supports bilingual display with English translation, "Translated by AI" badge, and original isiZulu text.
  • Responsibility Block: Shows Department ("Water"), "Done by: Contractor", "Contractor: Sample Plumbing Services (Pty) Ltd", "Expected fix date: 18 Nov 2026", and "Estimated time to fix: 2 days".
  • Upvote Button: Verbatim "Affects me too" with dynamic count pill (14). Un-upvoted and upvoted states supported. Non-logged in visitors redirect to S3.
  • Status Timeline: Newest at top with status dot, date and time ("12 Nov 2026, 08:00"), and notes ("Parts ordered and crew booked").

State Coverage & Role-Based Actions (PRD 8.2 & 7.2)

  • Role Action 1 (Resident Reporter when Fixed): "Is this fault fixed?" with primary button "Yes, it's fixed" (→ Closed, toast "Thanks for confirming.") and secondary button "No, still a problem" (→ M4).
  • Role Action 2 (Ward Admin): Primary button "Assign" (Received, Viewed, Reopened) or "Reassign" (Assigned, Scheduled), showing "Municipal reference: JW-778812" and contractor phone number.
  • Role Action 3 (Field Team): Primary button "Update status" (→ M3).
  • Empty State: Correctly omitted per PRD Section 8.2 ("not needed, because a fault always has at least its Received row in the timeline").
  • Loading State: Exactly as specified: grey photo block and 4 pulsing grey skeleton lines with loading indicator.
  • Error State: Both PRD error variants implemented: "We couldn't load this fault. Try again." with "Try again" button, and 404 variant "We couldn't find this fault." with "Back to faults".
  • Offline State: Cached fault details remain visible, top shared grey offline banner ("You're offline. Changes will not be saved."), and action buttons disabled (#D0D5DD).
  • Success State: Displays the verbatim toasts: "Fault reported. Your reference number is FMK-2026-00125.", "Fault assigned", and "Thanks for confirming."

Missing Items & TODOs:

All required elements, fields, labels, placeholders, errors, filters, and seed data states were fully and unambiguously specified in PRD Section 8.2, 8.3, 8.4, and 9.1. No missing elements or placeholder TODO boxes were required.
