S3 Log In Implementation & Specification Compliance Notes

Layout & Form Rules (PRD 8.2)

  • Dimensions: Exact 390 × 844 px phone frame with 16 px side margins and 8 px vertical grid rhythm.
  • Header (8.3): Heritage Brown (#531A17) with white back arrow (44px touch target) and 3 px Gold (#D8A851) underline. Title: "Log in".
  • Banner Area: Displays the three explicit PRD Section 8.2 messages:
    • "Check your email to confirm your account." (post-S2 arrival)
    • "Email confirmed. Please log in." (email verification link)
    • "Password changed. Please log in." (post-M1 reset)
  • Email Field: Required, placeholder "you@example.com". Exact error: "Enter a valid email address."
  • Password Field: Required, placeholder "Your password" with interactive show/hide toggle. Exact error: "Enter your password."
  • Forgot password?: Right-aligned link pointing to modal M1 with >44px touch target.
  • Primary Button: 48 px high Coat of Arms Green (#005D28), 8 px radius, text "Log in".
  • Create Account Link: Centered text "New to FixMyKasi?" with "Create account" pointing to S2.

State Coverage & Roles (PRD 8.2 & 7.2)

  • Normal State: Clean state displaying pre-filled sample content (zanele.k@example.com) and account confirmation banner.
  • Empty State: Marked as not needed in PRD Section 8.2 because the form itself constitutes the screen content.
  • Loading State: "Log in" button displays an animated spinner, replaces the label with "Logging in...", and is disabled.
  • Error State: Features all verbatim error messages:
    • "Wrong email or password."
    • "Too many attempts. Please wait a few minutes and try again."
    • "Please confirm your email first. Check your inbox." with actionable "Resend email" link.
  • Offline State: Top grey banner "You're offline. Changes will not be saved." with disabled "Log in" button (#D0D5DD / #667085).
  • Success State: Demonstrates redirect to the user's role home screen (S8 Ward Admin Queue for sample user Zanele Khumalo, Ward A), dynamically switching the bottom tab bar to Ward Admin: Queue, Dashboard, Faults, Profile.

Missing Items & TODOs:

All required elements, form fields, validation messages, error texts, banners, and state transitions were fully and unambiguously specified in PRD Section 8.2, 8.3, and 9.1. No missing items or grey placeholder TODO boxes required.
